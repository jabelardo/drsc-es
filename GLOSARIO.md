# Glosario EN → ES

Canonical Spanish terms for DRSC-ES, for everyone translating new material (see §7 of
`PLAN-FULL-PARITY.md`). Use one Spanish term per English term. If a term is missing or a
choice looks wrong, raise it before using an alternative.

- **Core terms** (below) are maintained by hand. Every entry reflects how `DRSC-ES.html`
  already uses the term.
- **Named game elements** (abilities, foci, descriptors, cyphers, artifacts, creatures,
  NPCs…) are generated from `DRSC-ES.html` by `tools/parity/glossary.py`. Re-run it after
  adding or renaming entries; `--check` reports whether this file is out of date.
- Game elements keep the English name in brackets: `Nombre en español [English Name]`.

### Alcance y distancia

Decided 2026-11-22. English uses two words on purpose: **range** for reach (attacks,
abilities, areas) and **distance** for movement and spacing. Keep the distinction: range is
always *alcance*, distance is always *distancia*, in the word order given in the table
above.

- **Prepositions:** *a corto / largo / muy largo alcance*, *dentro del alcance inmediato*,
  *de largo alcance* ("long-range"); *una corta distancia*, *hasta una larga distancia*.
- **Category names keep the noun first:** labels and definitions (*Distancia corta: es…*,
  *la distancia corta está cerca*) and table cells (*Alcance corto*).
- **Do not use *rango* for range.** *Rango* is a character's tier.
- **Non-game uses stay ordinary Spanish:** "close range", "long-haul" (*a corta
  distancia*, *de larga distancia*).

**Normalised 2026-11-22.**
- **Before:** the text used *distancia* for "short / long / very long range" about half the
  time, word order varied, and 19 phrases used *rango* for range.
- **The fix:** each phrase was checked against the English of the same entry or
  paragraph. Where neither existed, the Spanish wording decided (an article or a movement
  verb means distance), and the rest were reviewed by hand.
- **Result:** in the 1,700 entries that exist in both documents, the Spanish now agrees
  with the English in all but a handful of cases where the English itself mixes the terms.
- **ODT:** about 380 corrections are in `CORRECCIONES.json`, so the ODT gets the same
  terminology. That includes five entries where the range band itself was wrong (e.g.
  *Reubicar*, *Vampiro*).

### Decisiones tomadas

Settled 2026-11-22, before WP3:
- **training (noun):** split by meaning. The game term is *competencia*; the ordinary sense
  stays *entrenamiento*. The existing text was normalised: 32 occurrences, registered in
  `CORRECCIONES.json` as `training-01` to `training-28`.
- **Form (cypher/artifact field):** keep *Aspecto:*. It shares the word with "flavor"; context
  tells them apart.

Settled 2026-11-23, before WP7:
- **Sidekick:** *camarada*, distinct from *seguidor* (follower) and *compañero* (companion).
